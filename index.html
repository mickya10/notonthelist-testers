you're the bouncer · the rules change every night

Be one of the first players!

Not On The List is a fast, funny swipe game for Android. Read tonight's door rules, then swipe guests in or out in about a second each. A bell can change the rules mid-shift. Three wrong calls and you're fired!

  • 🚪 100 shifts in 5 venues: night club, castle, space airlock, Noah's ark, award show
  • 👽 Aliens in disguise, animals in pairs, VIPs who beat every rule
  • 📅 Daily Door: the same guests for everyone, keep your streak
  • 🕶️ 9 bouncers to collect · free, plays offline
